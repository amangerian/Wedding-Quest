Alex & Katherine

A love quest in three phases

Last updated: October 11, 2026 · First build — three phases, four heroes

How to Play

One button swaps between everyone you've found. Each hero opens up different paths, so come back to old places once you have new friends.

Move◀ ▶  /  A D  /  arrow keys
Jump⤒  /  Space, Z or ↑
Power✦  /  X or Shift
Swap heroFace button  /  C, Q or Tab
MapM  ·  Pause: Esc or P

Alex — swing the stethoscope from glowing rings (✦ to grab, Jump to let go, ↑ ↓ to reel).
Katherine — rewrite the system and flip gravity (stand on something first).
BA — knock things off ledges. They make great steps, and heavy things press switches.
Mikey — butt bump cracked rock: dash on the ground, slam down from the air.
Cats are small and fit through tiny gaps and pet doors.

Game controllers work too.
